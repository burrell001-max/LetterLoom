What the differences mean in practice

J is worth 10, not 8. A J word is worth more in Wordfeud and Words With Friends than in Scrabble, so a J on the rack is a bigger asset. Short J plays such as JO, JA and JAB are worth hunting for.

U costs 2. In Wordfeud the U is not a throwaway one-pointer, so dumping it is slightly less attractive than in Scrabble. Words that use it still score a little better.

The bingo bonus is 40. Using all seven tiles in Wordfeud earns 40 bonus points, against 50 in Scrabble. Bingos are still worth chasing, but a strong non-bingo play on a premium square can compete with them more often than in Scrabble.

Dictionaries differ. Wordfeud uses its own word list. If a rare word is rejected in the app, that is the dictionary, not your spelling. Check close calls in the game itself.

Frequently asked questions

How many points is a blank tile worth in Wordfeud?

Zero. A blank can stand in for any letter but scores nothing, which is why it is mainly valuable for making bingos.

Which letters are worth 10 in Wordfeud?

J, Q and Z. X is worth 8 and K is worth 5.

Is the Wordfeud bonus for using all seven tiles 50 points?

No. It is 40 points in Wordfeud, compared with 50 in Scrabble.

Can I use Scrabble word lists for Wordfeud?

Mostly, yes, since both accept a large common vocabulary, but each game has its own dictionary. Rare words may be accepted in one and not the other.
