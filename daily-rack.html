How scoring works

  • Each word is scored with Scrabble tile values, with no bonus squares.
  • Using all seven tiles adds the 50-point bingo bonus.
  • Each word counts once. Your score is the total of all words you find.
  • Words must be in our dictionary (ENABLE). Racks never include blanks.

A new rack appears every day at midnight in your local time. Want to practise with your own letters? Use the word unscrambler or the bingo finder.
