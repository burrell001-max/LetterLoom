Wordle Solver and Crossword Pattern Helper

Enter what you know about the hidden word. The helper filters the dictionary and ranks the remaining words by how useful a guess they are.

How to use it

  1. Pick the word length (5 for Wordle).
  2. Type each green letter in its box. Leave the others blank.
  3. Type yellow letters under the position where you saw them. A yellow letter must be in the word, just not there.
  4. Type gray letters in the last box.
  5. Use the best guesses list: it ranks words by how common their letters are among the remaining candidates, so a good guess removes as many options as possible.

Good to know

  • This also works as a crossword and pattern helper: fill in the letters you know and leave the rest blank.
  • Our list contains every word in the ENABLE dictionary, which is larger than the list of words Wordle actually uses as answers. Obscure words may appear; ranking favours common letters, not common words.
  • If a letter appears as both green or yellow and gray (a repeated letter in your guess), it is treated as present, and the helper does not limit how many times it appears.
