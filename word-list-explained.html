Our Word List Explained: What Is ENABLE?

By Kent Williams · Updated November 14, 2026

Every unscrambler is only as good as its dictionary. Here is exactly what ours contains and where it differs from the lists used in official games.

What ENABLE is

ENABLE stands for Enhanced North American Benchmark Lexicon. It is an open word list that was assembled for word games and has been widely used in software since the 1990s. Because it can be used freely, it is a common base for word tools.

What we load

The solver loads 168,567 words (the ENABLE list plus 16 modern additions such as QI, ZA, OK and EW), from two to fifteen letters long, directly into your browser. That is why results appear instantly and nothing is sent to a server. The counts by length are:

How it differs from official lists

  • Scrabble (North America). Tournament play uses the NASPA Word List, which is updated periodically. ENABLE lacks newer additions such as QI, ZA and OK, so we add a small set of them. Other recent additions may still be missing.
  • Collins Scrabble Words. Used in most countries outside North America and considerably larger, with many extra words.
  • Words With Friends and Wordscapes. Each uses its own list, which can include or exclude words that ENABLE treats differently.

What this means for you

For everyday play and puzzles, the list is large and reliable. For competitive play, check any word you are unsure about in your game's official dictionary. If a word you know is valid is missing from the solver, it is probably a recent addition to official lists.

Definitions

When you tap a word, the solver shows a short definition from a built-in dictionary where available, or from an online dictionary service otherwise. See the Privacy Policy for details.
