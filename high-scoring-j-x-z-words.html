High-Scoring J, X and Z Words

By Kent Williams · Updated November 14, 2026

J and X are worth 8 points and Z is worth 10 in Scrabble. Short words that use them are the easiest way to score big without needing a perfect rack.

The lists below come from our dictionary. Small numbers are Scrabble tile points before any premium squares.

Using them well

  • Put the tile on a double or triple letter square, and make sure it also sits in a second word. A Z on a triple letter is 30 points before the rest of the word.
  • Prefer plays that use the heavy tile and leave a balanced rack of vowels and consonants.
  • X is the most flexible of the heavy tiles because of AX, EX, OX, XI and XU.
  • J is harder: with only JO as a two-letter option here, you usually need a vowel ready on the board.

Words With Friends values differ slightly: J is 10 there instead of 8. See Scrabble vs Words With Friends.
