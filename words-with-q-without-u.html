Words With Q but No U

By Kent Williams · Updated November 14, 2026

The Q is worth 10 points in Scrabble and Words With Friends, but it is hard to place because almost every English Q word needs a U. These words do not.

Dictionary note

The most famous Q-without-U word, QI, is accepted in current official North American Scrabble dictionaries but is not in the ENABLE list this site uses, so the solver will not show it. If you are playing Scrabble or a similar game that accepts it, QI is the easiest way to get rid of a Q. Always confirm in your own game's dictionary.

How to play a Q without a U

  • Learn the short ones. QAT, QATS, QOPH, QAID and QANAT are the practical choices from this list. Longer entries such as QINDAR, QINTAR and TRANQ are rare but legal in this list.
  • Use a blank. Type a ? into the solver for a blank tile to see more Q options.
  • Look for the U first. If you can keep a U or an open U on the board, you have far more words. Words such as QUIZ, QUOTA and QUEEN become options once a U is available.
  • Exchange early. In Scrabble, if you have a Q and no U or blank and cannot play it for a reasonable score, exchanging tiles can be better than holding it for several turns.

Q words that need a U

With a U or a blank tile, many more high-scoring plays open up. Enter your rack, including a ? for a blank, into the solver to see them ranked by points.
