Wordscapes Tips and Tricks

By Kent Williams · Updated November 14, 2026

Wordscapes is a crossword-style game where you build words from a wheel of letters. Words of three or more letters count.

Core tips

  1. Start with the short words. Three- and four-letter words fill the crossing squares and give you letters for the longer answers.
  2. Look for plurals and endings. If you find CAT, check CATS. If you find WALK, check WALKED and WALKING when the letters allow.
  3. Shuffle often. Rearranging the wheel changes how you see the letters.
  4. Use the longest word first when you can see it. The puzzle is designed around one long word that uses most of the letters.
  5. Bonus words. Valid words that do not fit the grid usually count as bonus words and can earn rewards, so try anything that looks right.

Using the solver

Type the letters from the wheel into the solver and select the Wordscapes tab. It lists words of three or more letters, longest first. Use the length chips to narrow to the number of squares in the gap you are stuck on, and Starts with or Contains to match letters you already have in the grid.

A note on dictionaries

Wordscapes has its own accepted words, so a word from our list might not be accepted, and the reverse. Treat the solver as a strong guide and not a guarantee.
