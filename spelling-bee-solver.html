How the rules work

  • Words must have four or more letters.
  • Every word must contain the center letter.
  • Words can only use the seven letters, and letters can repeat.
  • A pangram uses all seven letters at least once.
  • Typical scoring: a four-letter word is 1 point; longer words score one point per letter; a pangram earns 7 extra points. We show these scores.

Likely answers and other valid words

Our dictionary is large and contains many obscure words that the puzzle itself does not accept. We split results into likely answers (common words that have a definition in our dictionary) and other valid words. The official game uses its own curated list, so a word we show may be rejected, and a rare word it accepts may be missing.
