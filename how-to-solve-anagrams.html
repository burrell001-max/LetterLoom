How to Solve Anagrams Faster

By Kent Williams · Updated November 14, 2026

An anagram is a word made by rearranging all the letters of another word. Solving them quickly is a skill you can learn.

Techniques

  1. Spot the endings. Look for -S, -ED, -ER, -ING, -LY and -EST first. Set them aside and solve the rest.
  2. Find common clusters. TH, CH, SH, ST, TR, PR, QU and ING are common. Group them and the remaining letters often fall into place.
  3. Pair vowels and consonants. Try different consonants as the first letter, then see which vowels follow.
  4. Rearrange visually. Write the letters in a circle or on separate tiles. Seeing them in a new order helps your brain stop reading the jumble.
  5. Work by length. Find short words first and combine them into longer ones.
  6. Check with the solver. Use the Anagrams tab, which lists only words that use all your letters.
