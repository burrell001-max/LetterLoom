WWordUnscramblerPro

About

WordUnscramblerPro is a free word unscrambler and anagram solver built for people playing Scrabble, Words With Friends, Wordscapes and other word games, and for anyone who likes puzzles.

Why it exists

Most word-helper sites are slow, cluttered or make you wait for a server. This one runs entirely in your browser: type your letters and the answers appear immediately, even on a phone.

What it does

  • Unscrambles any set of letters, including blank tiles (? or *).
  • Shows Scrabble and Words With Friends tile points for each word.
  • Filters by length, starting letters, ending letters, or letters contained.
  • Gives a short definition when you tap a word.

Our word list

We use ENABLE, an open-source dictionary of about 168,000 words. It is very close to, but not identical to, the official Scrabble dictionaries, so always confirm a disputed word in your game's own dictionary.

Who runs it
