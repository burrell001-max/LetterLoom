Word Cookies Tips: How to Find Every Word Faster

Word Cookies gives you a handful of letters and asks for every word hidden in them. There are no tile points, so the goal is simple: find them all. These habits help most.

1. Find the longest word first

The biggest word often uses every cookie letter, and the shorter words are frequently built from the same letters. Once you have the long one, you can peel smaller words off it by dropping letters or rearranging chunks.

2. Hunt for common endings and beginnings

Try adding S, ED, ER, ING, LY and RE to words you already have, and check starts like UN, RE and IN. A single base word can quickly become three or four answers.

3. Work through pairs

If you are stuck, pick two letters that sit well together (such as TH, ST, CH or SH) and ask what words start with them using the remaining letters. Systematic beats staring.

4. Rearrange the letters physically

Sliding letters into a new order is one of the best ways to see a word. Put vowels in the gaps between consonants, then try a different arrangement if nothing appears.

5. Check letter counts

A word is only possible if you have enough copies of each letter. If you have one E, a word needing two is out, which saves you from wasting guesses.

Example

Letters: A E I N R S T. Try the long words first, then drop a letter or two to get shorter ones. The solver will show everything these seven letters can make, grouped by length.

Use the solver when you are truly stuck

Type your cookie letters into the solver and choose the Word Cookies tab. It lists words of three or more letters, longest first, so you can see what is left to find. Word Cookies uses its own word list, so a very rare word from the solver may not be accepted by the game.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum word length in Word Cookies?

Words are three letters or longer, which is how the solver's Word Cookies tab filters results.

Does Word Cookies score points per letter?

There are no tile values like in Scrabble. The aim is to find the words in each puzzle.

Why did the game reject a word the solver showed?

The solver uses a large open dictionary, while the game uses its own list. Rare or unusual words may be missing from the game.

Is using a solver cheating?

That is up to you. Many players use it only to find the last word or two, or to learn new words after finishing a level.
