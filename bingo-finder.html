What is a bingo?

In Scrabble, playing all seven tiles from your rack in one turn earns a 50-point bonus, called a bingo. Words With Friends rewards it with a smaller bonus. Finding bingos is the biggest single skill that separates strong players from casual ones.

How to read the results

  • Uses all your tiles: words that use every letter on your rack. With a seven-tile rack these are bingos.
  • Add one more letter: for each possible extra letter, the longer words you could make. With a seven-tile rack that letter is a tile already on the board (an eight-letter bingo played through it). With six tiles it is the tile you would need to draw for a seven-letter bingo.
