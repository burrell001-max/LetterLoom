Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

LetterLoom is a free word unscrambler and anagram solver. This policy explains what information is and is not collected when you use the site. We have no accounts, sign-ups or forms that ask for personal details.

Letters you type stay in your browser

Solving happens entirely on your device. The letters, filters and word list choices you enter are not sent to our servers. The page address may update with your letters (for example #aeinrst) so you can bookmark or share a rack; that part of the address stays in your browser and is not sent to the site when the page loads.

Data stored on your device

  • Theme preference. We save your light or dark choice in your browser's local storage.
  • Cached files. A service worker may store the site's pages and word lists so it loads faster and works offline.
  • Consent choice. If a cookie banner is shown, we remember your answer in local storage.

You can clear these at any time from your browser's site settings.

Word definitions

When you tap a word, the site first checks its own built-in definitions. If none exists, it may request a definition from the free third-party service api.dictionaryapi.dev. That request contains the word you tapped and, as with any web request, your IP address and browser details are visible to that service. We do not send your full rack of letters.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs such as IP addresses and request details. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Advertising and cookies

We may show advertisements provided by Google AdSense. If ads are shown on the site:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalized ads are used, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

We do not control the cookies set by advertisers. If you decline advertising cookies, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

Children

LetterLoom is a general-audience word tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in a region with data protection rights and believe we hold data about you, contact us and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
