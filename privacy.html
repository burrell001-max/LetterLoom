Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what WordUnscramblerPro (“we”, “the site”) does with information when you use it. Site owner: Kent Williams, WordUnscramblerPro@outlook.com.

Letters you type

Word solving happens entirely in your browser. The letters and filters you enter are not sent to our servers or stored by us. The letters are placed in the page address after a # symbol so you can share or bookmark a search; that part of the address is not sent to the server when the page loads.

Information stored on your device

We use your browser's local storage only to remember your light or dark theme choice and, on the Daily Rack page, the words you found that day. It stays on your device.

Definitions

When you tap a word, the site may request its definition from a third-party dictionary service (dictionaryapi.dev). That request includes the word you tapped and your IP address, as with any web request, and is governed by that service's own practices.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs such as IP addresses and browser details; see GitHub's privacy statement for how it handles them.

Analytics and advertising

We may use analytics tools to understand how the site is used, and may display advertising, including Google AdSense. If we do, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads or aboutads.info. Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies. This policy will be updated when these services are added.

Children

The site is a general-audience word game tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we do not collect personal data directly, there is generally nothing to delete, but you can contact us at WordUnscramblerPro@outlook.com with any request.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
